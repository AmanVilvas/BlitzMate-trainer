Hero Philosophy

Instead of showcasing a product,

showcase the game itself.

The first screen begins from a bird's-eye view of a handcrafted wooden chessboard.

Only the center of the board is illuminated.

Everything else fades into darkness.

At the center rests a single ivory Staunton pawn.

The pawn slowly rotates and gently floats above the board.

As the user scrolls,

the camera slowly descends toward the pawn until it dominates the entire screen.

The chessboard disappears into darkness.

The pawn becomes the visual bridge into the rest of the website.
